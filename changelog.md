- Redesigned UI.
- Durations in minutes and seconds, via −/+ or direct input.
- Cycles and rest between cycles; rounds renamed to sets.
- Settings screen shows the total session duration.
- Timer: phase colors, progress bar, set/cycle dots, total time left, next-phase pill, skip button, end screen.
- 5-second countdown beeps.
- Embedded fonts for offline use (Bebas Neue for digits, Figtree for text).
